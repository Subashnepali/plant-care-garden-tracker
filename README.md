Plant Care and Garden Tracker Database
1. Application

My project will be a Plant Care and Garden Tracker Database. I chose this idea because people sometimes forget to water their plants or do not know how to take care of them properly.

This database will help users keep information about their plants, watering schedules, and plant health in one place. Users will be able to check when a plant needs water and keep records of the care they give to each plant. The goal is to make taking care of plants easier and help people keep their plants healthy.

2. Entities

The main entities I plan to include are:

Users: Stores information about people using the system.
Plants: Stores information about each plant, including its name and type.
Plant Categories: Groups plants into categories such as indoor plants, outdoor plants, flowers, and herbs.
Watering Schedules: Keeps track of when plants need to be watered.
Care Records: Records activities such as watering, adding fertilizer, and pruning.
Plant Health Records: Stores information about plant health problems and changes in their condition.

These entities will help organize the information and make it easier to manage different plants.

3. Business Processes

The database will support the following activities:

Adding a new plant to a user's collection.
Recording the type of plant and its care needs.
Creating and updating watering schedules.
Recording when a plant has been watered or received other care.
Checking previous care records.
Recording plant health problems and tracking changes over time.

This database will help users stay organized and remember the care their plants need.
